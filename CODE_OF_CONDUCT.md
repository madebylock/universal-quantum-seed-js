# Code of Conduct

Lock projects welcome technically rigorous participation from people of all
backgrounds. Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code,
not people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, publication of private information, sexualized conduct, and using
project spaces to pressure others into financial activity.

Security reports, personal disputes, and conduct reports must be handled
privately through an established maintainer channel. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary to keep
project spaces safe and productive.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and official project communication spaces. Good-faith disagreement and direct
technical feedback are welcome when they remain professional.
